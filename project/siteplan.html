Explore DRCongo

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Explore DRCongo

The name Explore DRCongo was chosen because it clearly represents the purpose of the website: helping visitors discover and explore the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

The name is simple, memorable, and directly connected to tourism, travel, culture, nature, and adventure in the DRC.

Optional domain: exploredrcongo.com

Site Purpose

Explore DRCongo is a tourism and travel website designed to help visitors discover some of the most interesting destinations in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

The website will provide information about major cities, tourist attractions, natural wonders, hotels, travel experiences, and places visitors can explore throughout the country.

Visitors will be able to discover destinations such as Kinshasa, Lubumbashi, Goma, and Kisangani. The site will also provide useful information that can help travelers plan their adventures and learn more about the culture and natural beauty of the DRC.

Scenarios

The target audience includes tourists, international visitors, local travelers, and anyone interested in discovering the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

  • What are the best destinations to visit in the Democratic Republic of the Congo?
  • What tourist attractions and activities can I find in cities such as Kinshasa, Lubumbashi, Goma, and Kisangani?
  • Where can I find information about hotels and places to stay during my trip?
  • How can I plan or reserve a trip to explore tourist destinations in the DRC?

Color Schema

The website will use colors inspired by nature, tourism, and the landscapes of the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Dark Green

#16423C

This color will be used for headings, the header, the footer, and important interface elements.

Light Green

#C4DAD2

This color will be used for section backgrounds, highlights, and secondary visual elements.

Gold

#DDA853

This accent color will be used for buttons, links, and important call-to-action elements.

White

#FFFFFF

White will be used for the main page background and text displayed on dark backgrounds.

Typography

Poppins

Explore the Democratic Republic of the Congo

Roboto
