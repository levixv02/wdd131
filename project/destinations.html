Explore Top Destinations

Discover the natural beauty, culture, history and adventure that make the Democratic Republic of the Congo one of Africa's most exciting destinations.

Kinshasa

The capital city known for music, nightlife, culture, museums and the magnificent Congo River.

Goma

Home to Lake Kivu and Mount Nyiragongo, one of Africa's most famous volcanoes.

Lubumbashi

A modern city rich in history, museums, wildlife parks and cultural heritage.

Kisangani

Famous for Boyoma Falls and beautiful tropical landscapes.

Must See Attractions

Virunga National Park

Discover gorillas, mountains, volcanoes and exceptional wildlife.

Lake Kivu

Relax and enjoy spectacular views on one of Africa's most beautiful lakes.

Boyoma Falls

One of the largest waterfall systems in the world.
